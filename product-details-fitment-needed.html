PRODUCT INFORMATION

034 High Output Ignition Coil EA8XX Engines - Red - Single

The 034Motorsport Ignition Coils are designed to be a Higher Output, direct plug-and-play upgrade from OE ignition coils with improved heat dissipation via the integrated CNC-Machined heat sinks and offer unique, colorful styling.

The 034Motorsport High Output Ignition Coils offer a 10% increase in energy output, which is perfect for those looking to maximize output from their stock or modified cars. With integrated CNC-machined heat sinks, any excess heat can be transferred from the ignition coil, further improving the performance of the coils. Crucially, 034Motorsport High Output Ignition Coils are available in red to show everyone at the local car meet that you value performance in the streets, the sheets, and the track!

For 6 Cylinder applications like the B9/B9.5 EA839 3.0T and 2.9TT engines, the factory engine harness sits atop the factory ignition coils, reducing airflow across the coils. With the selection of our Pack of 6, we include Billet Aluminum harness bracket spacers to raise the harness off of the ignition coils without adding strain to the wiring harness, allowing for proper space between the wiring harness and the heat sinks.

For 4 Cylinder, 5 Cylinder, and 8 Cylinder applications like the 4M.5, 4M8, C8, and D5 4.0TT engines, harness bracket spacers are not required.

Features:

  • 10% Increase in Energy Output over Stock Ignition Coils
  • CNC-Machined Heat Sink for Better Heat Dissipation
  • Red Housing with 034 Logo
  • Direct Plug-And-Play
  • Engine Harness Mounting Spacers included with Pack of 6 for V6 engines to Allow for Proper Airflow Across Heat Sinks (*Must Select Pack of 6 for these to be included, required for all EA839 V6 engines!*)
