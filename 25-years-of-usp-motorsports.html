25 YEARS AND FOUR WORLD RECORDS LATER

Welcome to USP Motorsports, a company built by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts. Our passion for cars and commitment to excellence has made us a leading provider of aftermarket upgrades for German and European vehicles.

Our story began in 2000, founded as a modest repair facility out of Coral Springs, Florida, specializing in Volkswagen and Audi vehicles. We became heavily involved in racing events, where we went on to set several world records. It was out of this passion for racing and the proven performance that we brought to the track, that we decided to start our online shop nearly 20 years ago and offer the same great products and services to people all over the world. We quickly gained a reputation for providing high-quality products and exceptional customer service.

At USP Motorsports, we are dedicated to providing our fellow enthusiasts with the best possible experience. Our team is made up of automotive geeks and enthusiasts who share the same passion as our customers, and our goal is to help you get the most out of your driving experience. We offer a wide range of OEM and performance upgrades to have you stanced out at a show to slaying at the track. Whichever direction you go we're ready to help you take your driving experience to the next level.

Our commitment to excellence and innovation is reflected in our world-record setting Audi B5 S4 VR6T, which we built in-house. This car has set multiple world records in its class, including a World Record pass of 8.68 at 166 mph. Our team of skilled enthusiasts worked tirelessly to create a vehicle that not only performs at the highest level but also looks stunning. The B5 S4 is a testament to our commitment to excellence and innovation in the automotive industry.

At USP Motorsports, we believe that cars are more than just a means of transportation – they are a way of life. We are passionate about helping our fellow enthusiasts get the most out of their driving experience. Our showroom and tuning facility in Coral Springs, Florida, is equipped with the latest technology and staffed by a team of dedicated professionals. We offer free shipping on all orders over $49, and our knowledgeable customer service team is available to answer your questions and provide technical support.

We are also proud to be part of the automotive enthusiast community, sponsoring events and participating in motorsports competitions. We believe that our shared passion for cars is what brings us together, and we are dedicated to supporting and engaging with our fellow enthusiasts.

Thank you for considering USP Motorsports for your automotive performance needs. Whether you are looking to upgrade your vehicle's performance, or simply looking for advice and support, we are here to help. As a company built by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts, we are committed to delivering the best possible experience and helping you achieve your performance goals.
