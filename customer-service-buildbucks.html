How It Works

At USP Motorsports, we value our customers and want to show our appreciation for your continued support. That's why we offer our all new BuildBucks Loyalty Rewards program to our registered users. Buy parts, earn BuildBucks, and spend them on more go-fast parts!

Create an Account

Join the USP Motorsports BuildBucks Loyalty Program by simply creating a free account on our website. It's quick and easy!

Earn BuildBucks

Shop for performance parts, engage with us on social media, refer friends, and more to earn BuildBucks points with every action. The more you do, the more points you accumulate!

Check Your Balance

Track your balance anytime by visiting your BuildBucks dashboard within your account.

Redeem Rewards

You can conveniently redeem your BuildBucks at checkout. When you're ready to make a purchase, head to the checkout page and apply your points!

ELIGIBILITY

All registered users of our website are automatically enrolled in the loyalty rewards program.

EARNING POINTS

Customers can earn points in a variety of ways, such as making purchases, referring friends, writing product reviews, and following our social media accounts. Points for orders placed are awarded 30 days after the order ships. All other points can be redeemed 14 days after an alternative method of earning (social tag, special promotion, etc). Earning multipliers may vary.

POINT REDEMPTION

Customers can redeem their points for discounts on future purchases, free shipping, or other rewards that may be offered from time to time. Redemption rates and rewards will be shown at checkout.

POINTS EXPIRATION

Points earned through the BuildBucks loyalty rewards program expire after two years from the date earned unless otherwise stated.

POINT ADJUSTMENTS

In rare cases, points may be adjusted due to fraud, returns, cancellations, or other reasons.

We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the BuildBucks loyalty rewards program at any time, with or without notice.
